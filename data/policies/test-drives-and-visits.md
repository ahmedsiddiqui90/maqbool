# Test Drives & Site Visits

## Booking a Test Drive
- Test drives are available on all car listings, Monday to Saturday, 10am to 6pm.
- A valid CNIC must be shown at the dealership before the test drive starts.
- Test drives last up to 20 minutes and are accompanied by a sales representative.

## Booking a Site Visit
- Property site visits can be scheduled any day, 9am to 7pm.
- Visits are free of charge and do not require a deposit.
- The dealer sends a confirmation message once a visit is booked, and a reminder 1 hour before.

## Cancellation
- Visits can be cancelled or rescheduled up to 2 hours in advance with no penalty.
- Cancelling with less than 2 hours' notice may require rebooking a new slot.
