# Extended Service Plans

Maqbool occasionally offers extended service plans for high-value purchases.

## Eligibility
- Available for cars under 5 years old and property purchased within the last 90 days.
- Ask your sales representative for the current extended-plan pricing sheet.

## Coverage
- Extended plans cover electrical and mechanical faults not covered by the standard warranty.
- Claims are filed through the same service desk as standard warranty claims.
