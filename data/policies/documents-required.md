# Documents Required

## For a Car Purchase
- Original CNIC (buyer, and guarantor if financed)
- Proof of income: salary slip or bank statement, last 3 months
- Two passport-size photographs

## For a Property Purchase
- Original CNIC
- NTN (National Tax Number) certificate
- Proof of funds or a financing pre-approval letter
- A recent utility bill for address verification

## For Financing
- 6 months of bank statements
- Employment letter or business registration documents
- Post-dated cheques, as required by the partner bank
