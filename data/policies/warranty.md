# Warranty

## Cars
- Used cars under 3 years old come with a 3-month/5,000 km dealer warranty covering engine
  and transmission.
- Cars over 3 years old are sold as-is, with no dealer warranty, unless stated otherwise in
  the listing.
- Any remaining manufacturer warranty transfers to the new owner at no extra cost.

## Property
- New-construction units come with a 1-year structural warranty from the builder.
- Resale properties are sold as-is; buyers are encouraged to get an independent inspection
  before purchase.

## Claims
- Warranty claims must be raised within the coverage period and are handled by the dealer's
  service desk, not by Maqbool directly.
