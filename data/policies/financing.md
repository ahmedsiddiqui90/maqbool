# Financing & Bank Partners

Maqbool dealers partner with several banks for auto and home financing.

## Partner Banks
- Meezan Bank (Islamic financing, profit-rate structure, no riba)
- Habib Bank Limited (HBL)
- United Bank Limited (UBL)
- Bank Alfalah

## Standard Terms
- Minimum down payment: 20% of the listing price for cars, 30% for property.
- Loan tenure: 1 to 7 years for cars, up to 20 years for property.
- Markup rate: starts at 18% per annum for conventional financing; Islamic financing uses a
  profit-rate structure instead of interest.
- Processing fee: 1% of the loan amount, capped at PKR 50,000.

## Eligibility
- Minimum monthly income: PKR 80,000 for car financing, PKR 150,000 for property financing.
- A valid CNIC and at least 6 months of bank statements are required.
