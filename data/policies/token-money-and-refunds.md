# Token Money & Refund Policy

## Token Money
- A token amount of PKR 50,000 (cars) or PKR 200,000 (property) holds a listing for 7 days.
- Token money is adjusted against the final price at the time of full payment.

## Refunds
- Token money is fully refundable if the dealer cancels the deal or the listing becomes unavailable.
- If the buyer withdraws after the 7-day hold without cause, 50% of the token is refundable;
  the rest covers administrative costs.
- Refunds are processed within 10 working days to the original payment method.

## Non-Refundable Cases
- Token money is non-refundable if false information was provided during booking.
